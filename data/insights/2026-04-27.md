# Insights — 2026-04-27

## h-2026-04-27-1: Gastown title fatigue
Cohort retention sits at 18.7% across the season but lifted to 22.9% on the 6 long-forms
this week (vs 16.3% the prior week's gastown-series videos). Hypothesis: the homogeneous
"Gastown ..." prefix is creating browse-page CTR collapse. Vary prefixes for the next 3
long-forms; expect retention to lift on those specific videos.

## h-2026-04-27-2: Live format fatigue
The 2026-04-22 stream (`ySzhUkGbh_M`, "First impressions of Steve Yegge's Gas City w/
$200/mo Claude") pulled 386 views, +1 sub — subs/1K=2.59. Prior week's live streams
(same Gas Town + $200/mo Claude framing) converted at 8.07 subs/1K. Same format, three-fold
conversion collapse. Hypothesis: viewers who wanted that combo subscribed in prior weeks;
repeating the format produces diminishing returns on existing audience. A topically novel
live (different framework, different workflow) should restore subs/1K above 5.

## h-2026-04-27-3: Shorts pipeline restart
Output dropped from 27 to 7 videos — almost entirely because shorts went to 0. Shorts
convert at 0.78 subs/1K (low) but are the channel's top-of-funnel: their absence is
visible in net subs (+14 → +2). Restart at 3+/day for 2 weeks; net weekly subs should
return to ≥14. If not, shorts are working harder than the per-1K rate suggests, OR
shorts have stopped working altogether. Either way the week's pause is the experiment.
