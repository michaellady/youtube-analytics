# Insights — 2026-04-27

## h-2026-04-27-1: Gastown title fatigue
The 4 gastown long-forms cited show retention decaying monotonically over time:
AAhPAyDl9xc (Jan 21) 51.8% → qxHeAX5KIjA (Feb 20) 45.0% → Ccd9j6DBJyw (Apr 3) 35.7%
→ vuiS_ofy12o (Apr 25, this week) 21.4%. Every title leads with "Gastown ..." or
"Gas Town ...". Hypothesis: homogeneous prefix is contributing to browse-page CTR
collapse, dragging retention with it. Vary the title prefix on the next 3 gastown
long-forms (lead with the verb, the outcome, or the surprise — not the brand) and
expect retention to lift 5pp above this week's 21.4% baseline.

## h-2026-04-27-2: Live format fatigue
Prior-week live `i59ozOROqd8` ("Compounding Taste + Distribution with $200/mo Claude
Code Skills") pulled 832 views, +7 subs → 8.41 subs/1K. This week's live `yoHHVgddUyI`
("First impressions of Steve Yegge's Gas City w/ $200/mo Claude") pulled 409 views,
+1 sub → 2.44 subs/1K. Same `$200/mo Claude` framing two streams in a row, 3.4x conversion
collapse. Hypothesis: subscribers who wanted that combo subscribed last week; repeating
the format produces diminishing returns. A topically novel live (different framework,
different workflow, no `$200/mo` prefix) should restore subs/1K to ≥5.

## h-2026-04-27-3: Shorts pipeline restart
Total channel output collapsed 31 → 2 videos week-over-week. Prior week ran ~9 shorts
across 4/15-4/17. This week: 0 shorts, 1 long-form, 1 live. Channel-wide net subs went
+10 → +2. Shorts convert poorly per-view (~0.7 subs/1K) but they are the top-of-funnel
that feeds longer-form discovery. Hypothesis: restart 3+/day shorts cadence for 2 weeks;
net weekly subs should return to ≥10. If it does, shorts pipeline pause was the cause.
If not, shorts are no longer working — either way, the resumption is the experiment.
